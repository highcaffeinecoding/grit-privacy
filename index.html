Grit: Gym Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Your workout data

Routines, workouts, sets, weights and settings are stored only on your device. Grit has no accounts and no servers that receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Anonymous usage analytics

Grit uses TelemetryDeck to count a small number of anonymous events, such as a workout being started or finished, or the subscription screen being shown. These events contain no name, email, advertising identifier or workout content, and cannot be linked back to you. They are not used to track you across apps or websites. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. Grit never sees your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Grit schedules rest timer alerts on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Grit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
