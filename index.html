Grit: Workout Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Your workout data

Routines, workouts, sets, weights and settings are stored only on your device. Grit has no accounts and no servers that receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Analytics

Grit contains no analytics or tracking code that sends data to us. If you choose to share app analytics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as installs and crashes.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. Grit never sees your payment details.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Grit runs a strength training workout in Apple Health while you train and saves finished workouts to Health. Grit does not read your Health data, and nothing is sent to a server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Grit schedules rest timer alerts on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Grit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
