Grit: Workout Tracker – Support

Contact

Email highcaffeinecoding@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

Subscriptions

Your first 3 workouts are free. Grit Pro starts with a 1-week free trial, then $29.99 a year or $4.99 a month. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open the paywall in Grit and tap Restore Purchases.

Rest timer alerts

Allow notifications for Grit so the rest timer can alert you with the phone locked. Apple Health is optional and saves finished workouts to Health.

Your data

Workouts are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.
